How to Play

Controls

  • Desktop: Use WASD keys to move
  • Mobile/Tablet: Swipe to move

Gameplay

  • Eat the bubble with the correct answer
  • Correct answers grow the snake and earn points
  • Wrong answers grow the snake by 1 and deduct points
  • Longer streaks earn bigger bonus points
  • Eat "P" items for wall penetration (3 moves)
  • Don't hit walls or yourself!
